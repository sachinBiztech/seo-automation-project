## Messaging Guardrails

### CAN say
- "BiztechCS has implemented Odoo for 200+ clients"
- "Our Odoo implementation team has 10+ years of experience"
- "We specialize in mid-market manufacturing ERP implementations"
- Specific client outcomes (with consent — use "a leading manufacturer" if no consent)
- Odoo is "cost-effective vs SAP/Oracle" (factually accurate)

### CANNOT say
- Specific client names without written consent
- Revenue figures (internal — not for publication)
- "We are the best Odoo partner" (unverifiable superlative)
- Claims about competitor quality or reliability
- "Guaranteed ROI" or "guaranteed results"
- Any mention of ongoing partnership disputes or legal matters

- Any claim comparing BiztechCS directly to a named competitor on quality
- Any new service area not in the active products list above
